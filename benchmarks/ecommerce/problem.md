# Specialty marketplace

We are launching a marketplace for small food producers. Sellers maintain products, photos,
availability, and shipping regions. Shoppers browse and search, keep a cart, apply offers, pay,
track delivery, cancel when allowed, and request refunds. Operations staff handle disputes and
correct seller mistakes.

Offers include percentage discounts, fixed discounts, “buy three get one,” first-order offers,
seller-funded offers, and free shipping. Some can combine and some cannot. A shopper should see
why a price changed. We expect new offer types monthly and country-specific tax and rounding
rules later this year.

At checkout, available units should be held for 15 minutes. We use one card company initially
but want a second next quarter. A slow card response must not result in two charges or an order
whose status nobody can explain. Sellers may fulfill items separately. Search can lag product
edits by up to five minutes, but a confirmed order must use the price and availability that
were accepted at checkout.

Launch traffic is estimated at 2,000 checkouts per minute with ten times that during campaigns.
Product browsing must remain available if reporting is delayed. We have not settled whether
the displayed cart price is promised until checkout, how partial seller cancellation affects
offers, or who absorbs rounding differences on split refunds.
