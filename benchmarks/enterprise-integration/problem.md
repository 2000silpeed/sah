# Acquired-company operations exchange

After three acquisitions, our manufacturing group has twelve systems holding customers,
products, orders, shipments, invoices, and account status. The same idea has different IDs,
field meanings, and status values in different companies. Plants cannot replace their local
systems for at least 18 months, but corporate teams need group-wide order visibility and
customers should not receive contradictory account or shipping information.

Some systems can send changes immediately, some support periodic queries, and two produce
nightly files. Transfers are duplicated, delayed, and occasionally delivered out of order.
A broken plant or bad product record must not stop unrelated companies. Operations need to
reprocess failures and explain where a corporate value came from.

Customer privacy choices must reach every applicable system within 24 hours. Invoice totals
must never be silently “fixed” during translation. Local plants may continue using a legacy
status after corporate reporting introduces a new one.

We have not agreed which system owns a customer address after an order is placed, whether
product changes flow both ways, or whether corporate order status should preserve every local
distinction. A fourth acquisition is likely next year. Teams want progress in quarterly slices,
not a multi-year replacement before any value appears.
